The median is a measure of central tendency that represents the middle value of a dataset when it is ordered from least to greatest. It is particularly useful for datasets that may have outliers or skewed distributions, as it is less affected by extreme values compared to the mean.

# Calculation of the Median

To calculate the median, follow these steps:

1. **Order the Data**: Arrange the data points in ascending order (from smallest to largest).
2. **Determine the Number of Data Points**: Count the total number of data points (n).
3. **Find the Median**:
   - If n is odd, the median is the value at the middle position.
   - If n is even, the median is the average of the two middle values.
